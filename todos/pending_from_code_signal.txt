### Key Insight to Internalize

The `structures[i] - i` trick generalizes broadly:

```
Ascending target:  H[0] + i  =>  normalize: structures[i] - i  =>  find max, sum gaps
Descending target: H[0] - i  =>  normalize: structures[i] + i  =>  find max, sum gaps
```

Once you normalize, *both branches reduce to*: **"how much do I need to raise a flat floor so everyone fits under it?"** — which is just `max(normalized) - normalized[i]` summed. That exact reduction also appears in 945, 453, and 2033.
