# Kubernetes Observability

## Key Signal Domains
- Control plane health.
- Node health and allocatable resources.
- Pod lifecycle and restart patterns.
- Workload latency/error saturation indicators.

## Practical Checks
- Restart spikes by namespace/workload.
- Node memory pressure and eviction signals.
- Pending pods due to scheduling/resource constraints.

## What I Can Say In Interview
- “I apply established monitoring fundamentals to Kubernetes signals, with focus on restart patterns, resource pressure, and service impact.”

## Do Not Overclaim
- Do not claim deep cluster SRE ownership if not yet held in production.
