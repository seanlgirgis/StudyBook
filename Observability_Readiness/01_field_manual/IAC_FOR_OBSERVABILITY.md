# IaC For Observability

## Why It Matters
- Consistency across environments.
- Reviewable monitoring changes.
- Repeatable alert/dashboard standards.

## Practical Pattern
- Define alarms/alerts in code.
- Enforce naming/tagging/owner conventions.
- Version control threshold changes.
- Peer review with runbook links.

## Terraform and CloudFormation Scope
- Start with simple cloud alarms and notifications.
- Expand to dashboards and policy-as-code.

## What I Can Say In Interview
- “I use IaC to reduce drift and improve observability governance, while continuing hands-on depth in specific toolchains.”

## Do Not Overclaim
- Do not claim extensive multi-year Terraform/CloudFormation production ownership unless accurate.
