# Capacity Forecasting Playbook

## Objective
Predict reliability risk before incidents through trend and headroom analysis.

## Inputs
- Utilization history.
- Growth trends.
- Seasonal patterns.
- Business demand drivers.

## Workflow
1. Baseline key utilization and latency metrics.
2. Calculate headroom and growth rate.
3. Flag time-to-risk windows.
4. Recommend scaling or optimization actions.
5. Report risk with confidence and assumptions.

## Outputs
- Capacity risk score by service/domain.
- Forecast horizon with confidence bands.
- Action plan and owner accountability.

## What I Can Say In Interview
- “I have repeatedly translated raw telemetry into forecasted risk and clear operational decisions.”
