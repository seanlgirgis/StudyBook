# Monday Morning Checklist

## 60-90 Minutes Before Interview
- Review 30-second opener and 2-minute expanded version.
- Skim five stories and two runbooks.
- Reconfirm truthful tool framing and no-overclaim language.
- Prepare 3 client questions about observability maturity and operational model.

## 30 Minutes Before
- Open notes for: talk track, role notes, Monday plan.
- Do one verbal rehearsal with timer.
- Check audio/video and meeting logistics.

## 5 Minutes Before
- Reset to operator mindset: detect, triage, mitigate, communicate.
- Enter with concise, outcome-oriented language.
