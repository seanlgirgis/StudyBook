# Monday Client Interview Plan

## Objective
Be interview-ready by Monday with clear, credible positioning and operationally strong examples.

## 72-Hour Plan
### T-72 to T-48 (Friday)
- Lock positioning statement and role-fit narrative.
- Review cloud observability map and must-know concepts.
- Draft interview-safe statements for Kubernetes, OTel, Prometheus/Grafana, GPU monitoring.

### T-48 to T-24 (Saturday)
- Rehearse 30-second, 2-minute, and 5-minute tracks.
- Run through 3 incident runbooks verbally.
- Prepare question set for client (observability maturity, on-call model, SLO ownership, tooling landscape).

### T-24 to T-12 (Sunday)
- Do final pass on stories with measurable impact.
- Create one-page “memory anchor” from key bullets.
- Practice concise responses to architecture and migration questions.

### T-12 to T-0 (Monday Morning)
- Execute checklist.
- Rehearse opening statement once.
- Enter interview with operator mindset: detect, triage, mitigate, communicate risk.

## What I Can Say In Interview
- “I bring deep APM and capacity engineering experience and map that directly to cloud-native observability patterns.”
- “My strength is converting telemetry into action: thresholds, forecasts, runbooks, and executive risk clarity.”

## Do Not Overclaim
- Do not claim deep production ownership of Kubernetes, GPU stack, Terraform/CloudFormation, OTel Collector, Prometheus, or Grafana.
- Position these as current ramp-up areas with transferable monitoring fundamentals.
