# Must Know Cloud Observability

## Core Model
- Metrics: trend and threshold detection.
- Logs: event-level detail and context.
- Traces: request path and latency breakdown.

## Minimum Architecture Fluency
- Instrumentation sources (app, infra, platform).
- Collection and transport (agent/collector).
- Storage and query layers.
- Alerting and incident workflow.
- Dashboards and stakeholder views.

## Priority Concepts For Monday
- Golden signals: latency, traffic, errors, saturation.
- SLI/SLO basics and error budgets.
- Alert quality: actionable, low-noise, owner-tagged.
- Correlation workflow across metrics/logs/traces.
- Capacity risk indicators: utilization trend, headroom, growth rate.

## What I Can Say In Interview
- “I optimize observability for actionability: fast triage, clear ownership, and measurable risk reduction.”
- “I connect reliability telemetry with capacity planning, not isolated dashboards.”

## Do Not Overclaim
- Do not present deep tool-internals expertise where unproven.
- Use “working knowledge + active implementation ramp” language for new tools.
