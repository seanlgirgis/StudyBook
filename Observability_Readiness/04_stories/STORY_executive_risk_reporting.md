# Story: Executive Risk Reporting

## Situation
Leadership needed clearer reliability and capacity risk visibility for planning and prioritization.

## Task
Create concise reporting that connected technical telemetry to business risk.

## Actions
- Built KPI summary views with trend and threshold context.
- Standardized risk language and confidence indicators.
- Added recommended actions and ownership signals.

## Results
- Better executive understanding of risk posture.
- Improved prioritization and accountability.

## What I Can Say In Interview
- “I bridge technical observability data and executive decision-making with clear, actionable risk narratives.”
