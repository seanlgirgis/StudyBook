# Story: 6,000 Endpoint Telemetry

## Situation
Environment required telemetry visibility across 6,000+ infrastructure endpoints with inconsistent signal quality.

## Task
Standardize telemetry and KPI workflows so operations could trust dashboards, thresholds, and downstream capacity decisions.

## Actions
- Consolidated telemetry definitions and KPI baselines across endpoint groups.
- Improved thresholding and exception reporting for better actionability.
- Built dashboard views that connected endpoint health, trend behavior, and operational risk.
- Tightened RCA workflows so teams could separate signal noise from real system stress.

## How This Supports Capacity Forecasting
- Large endpoint telemetry gives broader trend coverage and better early warning signals.
- Forecast quality depends on telemetry quality; cleaner data means fewer false risk calls.
- Host/application/service context from endpoint data improves feature reliability.

## Telemetry Quality -> Forecast Quality
- Timestamp consistency supports valid time buckets.
- Duplicate/stale record control prevents distorted trends.
- Threshold hygiene improves breach-flag signal reliability.
- Better RCA feedback helps refine risk bands and reduce false positives.

## Dashboards, Thresholds, and RCA Link To Capacity Planning
- Dashboards visualize trend and headroom movement over time.
- Thresholds provide objective breach markers for risk scoring.
- RCA findings explain whether risk is sustained trend or one-time anomaly.
- Combined, they create a practical decision loop for capacity actions.

## Results
- Faster anomaly detection and clearer triage pathways.
- Better alignment between operations telemetry and capacity planning conversations.
- More confidence in risk communication to management.

## What I Can Say In Interview
I led telemetry normalization and actionability at scale across 6,000+ endpoints, and that foundation directly improved the quality and trustworthiness of capacity forecasting decisions.
