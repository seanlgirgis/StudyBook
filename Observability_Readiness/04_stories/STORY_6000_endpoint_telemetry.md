# Story: 6,000 Endpoint Telemetry

## Situation
Environment required telemetry visibility across more than 6,000 endpoints with inconsistent signal quality.

## Task
Standardize KPI views and create actionable thresholding for operations.

## Actions
- Consolidated telemetry definitions and baseline metrics.
- Built tiered thresholds and exception reporting.
- Established operational dashboards and ownership mapping.

## Results
- Faster anomaly detection and clearer triage pathways.
- Improved confidence in reliability and capacity discussions.

## What I Can Say In Interview
- “I led telemetry normalization and actionability at scale, converting raw signal volume into operational decisions.”
