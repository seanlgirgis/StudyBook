# Story: Dashboard Thresholds

## Situation
Dashboards were visually rich but operationally noisy and hard to act on.

## Task
Increase alert-to-action quality using better threshold design.

## Actions
- Reworked thresholds around baseline behavior and business impact.
- Added ownership and runbook linkage.
- Removed low-value noise and duplicated signals.

## Results
- Higher signal-to-noise ratio.
- Better on-call focus and response consistency.

## What I Can Say In Interview
- “I optimize dashboards and alerts for operator decisions, not just visibility.”
