# Story: Capacity Forecasting

## Situation
Capacity concerns were identified late, increasing incident and performance risk.

## Task
Build repeatable forecasting and risk reporting to provide earlier action windows.

## Actions
- Developed trend and headroom models.
- Created KPI-based risk summaries for technical and executive audiences.
- Introduced recurring review cadence and owner accountability.

## Results
- Earlier risk visibility and better planning decisions.
- More consistent communication between engineering and leadership.

## What I Can Say In Interview
- “I translated telemetry trends into forecasted risk and concrete capacity actions.”
