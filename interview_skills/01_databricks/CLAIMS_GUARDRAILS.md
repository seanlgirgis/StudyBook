# Claims Guardrails (Databricks)

## Safe Claims
- "I have practical Databricks exposure and focused study."
- "My strongest foundation is Python, SQL, AWS, and Spark-style ETL."
- "I am comfortable with notebooks, SQL, and Delta table workflows."

## Caution Areas
- Long-term Databricks platform ownership.
- Heavy real-time streaming ownership at large scale.
- Deep platform admin claims unless directly validated by your history.

## Do Not Overclaim
- Multi-year Databricks architecture leadership.
- Advanced ownership in Azure, GCP, Kafka, Kubernetes, Terraform, or Power BI.
- End-to-end ML model science ownership if your role was data/pipeline support.

## Safe Alternatives
- "I supported ML workloads through reliable feature/data pipelines."
- "I can ramp quickly because my Spark and AWS fundamentals are strong."
- "I focus on data quality, monitoring, and operational reliability."
