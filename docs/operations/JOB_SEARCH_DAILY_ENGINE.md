# Job Search Daily Engine

**Date Registered:** 2026-04-03
**Owner:** Sean + Codex
**Cadence Goal:** 5-10 applications per day

## Mission

Run a consistent application + upskilling engine to break into Data Engineering quickly, with measurable daily output and interview readiness growth.

## Daily Throughput Target

- Pull and triage: 25 postings/day
- Apply: 5-10/day
- Mix:
  - 3-4 high-fit tailored applications
  - 2-4 medium-fit light-tailored applications
  - 1-2 fast applications when low friction

## Primary Boards

- LinkedIn Jobs
- Indeed
- Wellfound
- BuiltIn
- Dice
- ZipRecruiter
- Alaska/state and company career portals

## Core Search Strings

1. `(\"data engineer\" OR \"etl engineer\" OR \"analytics engineer\" OR \"data platform engineer\") AND (python AND sql) AND (aws OR snowflake OR databricks OR pyspark)`
2. `(\"data engineer\" OR \"data platform engineer\") AND (performance OR observability OR capacity OR telemetry) AND (python AND sql)`
3. `(\"data engineer\" OR \"etl engineer\" OR \"analytics engineer\") AND (alaska OR anchorage OR remote) AND (python AND sql)`
4. `(\"data engineer\" OR \"etl developer\") AND (contract OR c2c OR w2) AND (python AND sql AND aws)`

## Tithe Rule (Mandatory)

For each application submitted:
1. Extract one skill/topic from the JD.
2. Do one 10-15 minute training nugget on that topic.
3. Write 3 interview Q&A lines tied to that topic.

## Codex Per-Job Support Contract

For each JD provided, Codex returns:
1. Fit score (0-100)
2. Why fit + risks
3. Tailored bullets
4. Short custom cover note
5. Interview Q pack
6. Tithe training action

## Start Command (Human Trigger Phrase)

Say this exactly:

`Start Job Search Engine (today): target 7 applications.`

Optional variants:
- `Start Job Search Engine (today): target 10 applications.`
- `Start Job Search Engine (light day): target 5 applications.`

## End-of-Day Closeout

- Applications submitted count
- Follow-ups needed
- Top 3 interview topics from tithe tasks
- Plan for next day
