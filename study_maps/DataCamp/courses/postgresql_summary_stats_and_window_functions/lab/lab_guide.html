Practice Sequence

1. Foundations

ROW_NUMBER, ORDER BY, PARTITION BY, GROUP BY comparison.

2. Fetching and Ranking

LEAD, FIRST_VALUE, LAST_VALUE, RANK, DENSE_RANK, NTILE.

3. Aggregates and Frames

Running totals, moving averages, moving maximums, ROWS and RANGE.

4. Beyond Windows

CROSSTAB, ROLLUP, CUBE, COALESCE, STRING_AGG.
