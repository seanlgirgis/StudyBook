## Lab scope

The lab is a compact PostgreSQL design exercise. It creates a normalized operational schema, a dimensional analytical schema, reporting views, a materialized view, a read-only analyst role, and a range-partitioned event table.

The lab remains **DEVELOPING** until it is executed locally and evidence is recorded.
