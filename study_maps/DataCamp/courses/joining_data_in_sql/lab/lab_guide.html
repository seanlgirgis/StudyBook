What to notice

  • INNER JOIN can multiply rows.
  • LEFT JOIN filters behave differently in ON and WHERE.
  • FULL JOIN exposes unmatched rows from both sources.
  • CROSS JOIN row counts multiply.
  • A self join can be replaced by conditional aggregation in some reporting problems.
  • UNION removes duplicates; UNION ALL preserves them.
  • EXCEPT is directional.
  • NOT EXISTS is safer than NOT IN when NULL is possible.
