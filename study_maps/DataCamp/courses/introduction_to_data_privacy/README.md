## Course scope

This course covers:

- privacy and security foundations;
- the CIA triad and privacy/security overlap;
- Privacy by Design;
- data classification and privacy risk;
- pseudonymization, anonymization, masking, generalization, and aggregation;
- data lifecycle management, retention, and secure deletion;
- data brokers and user profiling;
- privacy laws, enforcement, penalties, and the jAsper framework;
- data sovereignty, vendor governance, emerging technology, and shifting privacy left.
