## Course purpose

This course builds practical judgment for responsible AI. It covers:

- fairness, accountability, and transparency;
- privacy, personalization, and human oversight;
- black-box models and Explainable AI;
- proxy variables and hidden bias;
- LIME, SHAP, and interpretable models;
- ethical frameworks and stakeholder participation;
- Ethical AI by Design;
- feedback, monitoring, and continuous improvement.

## Lab decision

This is a conceptual governance course. It does not require a code-patterns document or a large runnable programming lab.

The course-local lab is a light scenario-analysis and governance-design practice area. Use it to:

- identify affected stakeholders;
- discover possible harms and proxy variables;
- apply fairness, accountability, and transparency;
- compare LIME, SHAP, and interpretable-model choices;
- design feedback, appeal, monitoring, and escalation controls;
- create a simple ethical framework for an AI use case.
