# AI Ethics — Field Guide

## Course punchline

Responsible AI is not only accurate. It must be fair, understandable, accountable, appropriately controlled by humans, and monitored throughout its lifecycle.

## Core principles

- **Fairness:** Look for unjustified differences in outcomes and error rates.
- **Accountability:** Name the people and organizations responsible for decisions and corrections.
- **Transparency:** Explain data, preparation, limitations, use, and important decisions.
- **Human oversight:** Preserve review, override, escalation, and appeal for consequential decisions.
- **Privacy:** Collect only needed data, explain its use, and give people meaningful control.
- **Explainability:** Use interpretable models or explanation methods suited to the decision.

## Important distinctions

### Equal treatment versus fair outcome

Applying the same process to everyone does not guarantee fairness. Context, access, and outcomes matter.

### Sensitive attributes versus proxies

Removing race, age, gender, or another protected field does not guarantee fairness. ZIP code, graduation year, school, income, or other features may indirectly reveal similar information.

### Interpretable models, LIME, and SHAP

- **Interpretable model:** understandable by design.
- **LIME:** creates a simple local approximation for one prediction.
- **SHAP:** estimates how much each feature contributed to a prediction.

## Ethical AI by Design

1. Define responsible objectives.
2. Involve relevant stakeholders.
3. Collect and manage data responsibly.
4. Design for transparency and human control.
5. Test bias and group-level performance.
6. Provide feedback, complaint, review, and appeal channels.
7. Monitor, audit, and iterate after deployment.

## High-impact AI review

Ask:

1. Who is affected?
2. What harms are possible?
3. Is the data representative?
4. Are there proxy variables?
5. Can the decision be explained?
6. Who owns the outcome?
7. Can a qualified human override it?
8. How can an affected person challenge it?
9. How will performance and fairness be monitored?

## Quick memory rules

- Fairness checks the **outcome**.
- Accountability checks the **owner**.
- Transparency checks the **explanation**.
- Plan diversity early, verify it in the data, test it in outcomes, and monitor it after launch.
- Ethical AI is both risk protection and business advantage.
