Local Lab

AI Ethics is a conceptual governance course. The local lab uses scenario analysis rather than code. Practice by identifying stakeholders, harms, proxies, accountability owners, explanation needs, appeal paths, monitoring controls, and ethical trade-offs.

  • How to Use the Ethics Practice Lab
  • AI Ethics Scenario Run Book
  • Certification Preparation
  • Final Self-Test
No code-patterns document is required. Completion means being able to reason through AI ethics scenarios, explain trade-offs, assign responsibility, and propose practical safeguards.
