Analyzing Students' Mental Health

A completed DataCamp SQL project rebuilt locally in PostgreSQL with the original CSV, validated queries, a project field guide, quick lookup, practice SQL, and a reusable lab guide.

Project question
  • Keep international students only.
  • Group them by length of stay.
  • Count students in each group.
  • Compare average mental-health scores.

Validated result

The local PostgreSQL result matched the expected DataCamp output: 286 source rows, 201 complete international rows, 9 stay groups, and 9 final rows × 5 columns. 
stay
count_int
average_phq
average_scs
average_as
The result is descriptive. It shows how average scores vary across length-of-stay groups, but it does not prove that length of stay caused changes in mental health.

Core SQL pattern

SELECT
    group_column,
    COUNT(*) AS group_size,
    ROUND(AVG(metric), 2) AS average_metric
FROM table_name
WHERE row_filter
GROUP BY group_column
ORDER BY group_column DESC;

For this project: group_column = stay, row_filter = inter_dom = 'Inter', and the metrics are todep, tosc, and toas.
