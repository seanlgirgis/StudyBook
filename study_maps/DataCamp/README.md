# DataCamp StudyBook

This is the new canonical DataCamp learning product root under `study_maps\DataCamp`.

Old material is preserved in `study_maps\DataCamp.old`.

Content will be migrated bite by bite rather than as a big-bang move.

Courses and projects will become reusable canonical packages.

Tracks will become playlists and pointers that reference canonical course and project packages.

Runnable labs remain under `tutorials\DataCamp`.

## Career track placeholders created

Career track placeholder pages now exist under `study_maps\DataCamp\tracks`.

## Career tracks vs skill tracks

- Career tracks are job-path playlists.
- Skill tracks are focused skill bundles.
- Courses and projects remain canonical and reusable.
- Tracks should link to courses and projects, not duplicate them.
