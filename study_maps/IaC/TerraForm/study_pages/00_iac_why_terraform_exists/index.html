IaC: Why Terraform Exists

This orientation page explains the infrastructure landscape before Terraform internals.

Landscape Diagram

Before IaC

Infrastructure often relied on manual actions and undocumented practice. That worked at small scale but failed under growth pressure.

Why manual console work became risky

Click-ops is hard to review, repeat, and audit consistently across teams and environments.

Why scripts helped but were not enough

Scripts reduced manual effort but often lacked shared state, standard review, and unified desired-state modeling.

What IaC changed

Infrastructure moved into versioned, reviewable code workflows with clearer change control.

Why Terraform became popular

A consistent multi-platform desired-state workflow with plan/apply/state mental model.

Safe interview wording

IaC emerged because manual and loosely scripted workflows struggled with repeatability and scale. Terraform is a major option because it standardizes desired-state changes, while alternatives remain valid by context.

Common traps

  • Claiming console work is always wrong
  • Claiming Terraform is the only valid tool
  • Ignoring governance/process needs
