# Bridge Story

## 30-Second Version
My core background is data engineering, telemetry, monitoring, and capacity forecasting. Terraform helps me connect that operational world to repeatable cloud infrastructure workflow, so I can collaborate better with platform and cloud teams without overclaiming ownership.

## 2-Minute Version
I think of Terraform as infrastructure workflow discipline: define desired state, review plans, protect state, and apply changes safely. In my work, data and observability systems depend on storage, IAM, networking, and compute foundations. I use Terraform fluency to understand how those layers are defined and changed, then connect that with telemetry, dashboard design, alert quality, and capacity planning decisions.

## 5-Minute Version
My strength is operating data and observability-heavy environments: telemetry quality, monitoring clarity, capacity headroom, and management reporting. Terraform gives a shared language for how infrastructure is defined, reviewed, and changed safely. I use that language to bridge conversations between data engineering and platform teams. For example, if a storage, IAM, or compute pattern changes, I can reason about operational impact, expected telemetry shifts, alert implications, and capacity risk. I do not position myself as the primary Terraform platform owner, but I can contribute effectively to design reviews, plan discussions, and safer operational outcomes.
