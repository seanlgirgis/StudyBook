# Overclaim Guardrails

## Safe Claims
- I understand Terraform workflow fundamentals.
- I can connect Terraform changes to monitoring and capacity impact.
- I collaborate effectively with platform/cloud teams.

## Risky Claims
- I owned enterprise Terraform governance end-to-end.
- I was the main production Terraform platform engineer.
- I managed all AWS infrastructure with Terraform.

## Replacement Wording
- I built working fluency and can contribute to reviews and operational alignment.
- I understand safe team workflow even when platform teams own final apply paths.

## Interview Recovery Line
To be precise, I would not position myself as the primary Terraform platform owner. My strength is understanding the workflow and how it connects to data, monitoring, capacity, and cloud operations.
