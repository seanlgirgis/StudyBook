# Interview Answers

1. How does Terraform relate to AWS?
Terraform provides a repeatable way to define and review AWS resource configuration as code.

2. How does Terraform relate to observability?
It can version parts of observability config so dashboards/alerts are more reviewable and less manual.

3. How does Terraform relate to data engineering?
Data pipelines depend on infrastructure foundations Terraform can help define consistently.

4. How does Terraform relate to capacity planning?
Terraform defines infrastructure shape; capacity planning evaluates behavior, headroom, and trend risk.

5. What AWS resources might Terraform manage?
Common examples include storage, IAM roles, networking components, compute support, and monitoring assets.

6. How do dashboards or alerts become code?
Teams can define them in version-controlled config with review and ownership expectations.

7. How do you keep Terraform safe in teams?
Use PR review, plan review, state protection, locking, least privilege, and controlled apply paths.

8. What is your real Terraform experience?
I am building practical fluency and can connect Terraform workflow to my data/monitoring/capacity background.

9. How do you avoid overclaiming?
I separate what I have directly owned from what I understand and can collaborate on.

10. How would you collaborate with DevOps/platform teams?
I bring operational context, telemetry thinking, and capacity insights while aligning with platform safety workflows.
