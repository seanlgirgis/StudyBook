# Sean-Safe Positioning

Sean can safely say:
- I understand the Terraform workflow.
- I understand how Terraform supports repeatable cloud infrastructure.
- My strongest background is data engineering, telemetry, monitoring, capacity forecasting, and operational reporting.
- I am building Terraform fluency to collaborate better with cloud/platform teams.
- I understand plan, state, drift, providers, resources, variables, modules, environments, and safe team workflow.

Sean should not say:
- I owned enterprise Terraform production platforms.
- I was the primary Terraform platform engineer.
- I managed all AWS infrastructure with Terraform.
- I built production CI/CD Terraform governance from scratch.
