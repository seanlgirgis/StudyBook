## Map Design Rules

* Keep each map focused.
* Prefer 7 to 15 bubbles.
* Hard maximum is 21 bubbles.
* Use short definitions.
* Put longer explanations in study pages.
* Include common traps.
* Include safe interview sentences when helpful.
* Do not put raw transcripts in bubble cards.
