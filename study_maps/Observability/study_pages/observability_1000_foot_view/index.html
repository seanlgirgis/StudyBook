Observability 1000-Foot View

Observability is how modern teams understand what systems are doing by collecting and connecting telemetry signals.

Metrics

Numbers over time, such as CPU, memory, latency, errors, request count, disk usage, and queue depth.

Logs

Text records of events, errors, warnings, and system behavior.

Traces

A path of one request across services, showing where time was spent and where failure happened.

APM

Application Performance Monitoring focuses on application health, transactions, latency, errors, dependencies, and user experience.

Dashboards

Visual views that help humans quickly understand system behavior.

Alerts

Rules that notify people when something needs attention.

Runbooks

Step-by-step guidance for what to check and what to do when an alert or incident happens.

Capacity

Whether systems have enough CPU, memory, disk, network, throughput, or service headroom to handle demand.

Business Reporting

Turning technical signals into management-friendly risk, trend, forecast, and decision information.
