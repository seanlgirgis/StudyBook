Safe Interview Positioning

1. Strong Claims

  • I have enterprise monitoring experience.
  • I have APM-style operational experience.
  • I have worked with telemetry and system behavior.
  • I have worked with capacity monitoring.
  • I have built or supported dashboards and reporting.
  • I have worked with thresholds, headroom, risk, and forecasting.
  • I can explain how telemetry supports operational and business decisions.

2. Learning / Modernizing Claims

  • I am modernizing my monitoring background into cloud observability.
  • I am studying OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana, CloudWatch, Dynatrace, Splunk, Datadog, and Kubernetes monitoring.
  • I understand the conceptual role of metrics, logs, traces, dashboards, alerts, and runbooks.
  • I am building a portfolio to prove hands-on capability step by step.

3. Avoid Overclaiming

  • I owned an enterprise OpenTelemetry platform in production.
  • I was the production owner of Kubernetes observability.
  • I administered Datadog or Splunk Observability Cloud at scale.
  • I designed a full AWS observability platform alone.
  • I am a deep Grafana/Prometheus platform engineer already.

4. Best Short Positioning

My strongest background is enterprise capacity engineering and APM-style monitoring. I understand telemetry, dashboards, thresholds, forecasting, RCA, and management reporting. I am now updating that foundation into cloud-native observability patterns using OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana, CloudWatch, Dynatrace, Splunk, Datadog, and Kubernetes monitoring concepts.
