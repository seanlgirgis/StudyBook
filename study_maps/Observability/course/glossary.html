Level 0 Glossary

TermPlain-English MeaningSean Safe Sentence
ObservabilityUnderstanding system behavior by combining telemetry signals.I explain observability as connecting signals to understand health and risk.
TelemetryOperational data emitted by systems.I use telemetry as the evidence for monitoring and decision support.
SignalA data type used to observe systems, such as metrics, logs, or traces.I treat signals as inputs that must be interpreted in context.
MetricA numeric measurement over time.I use metrics to spot trends, thresholds, and headroom risk.
LogA timestamped text record of events or errors.I use logs to understand what happened around an issue.
TraceA path of one request across services.I use traces conceptually to locate where delay or failure occurred.
SpanA single timed step inside a trace.I understand spans as the building blocks of distributed tracing.
APMApplication performance monitoring for app health and user impact.My APM background helps me reason about modern observability workflows.
DashboardA visual summary of key system signals.I use dashboards to communicate operational state clearly.
AlertA notification triggered by a risk condition.I prefer alerts that point to action, not noise.
RunbookStep-by-step response guidance for incidents.I pair alerts with runbooks for repeatable response.
ThresholdA limit used to detect abnormal behavior.I use thresholds as guardrails, not as the only signal.
SLOAn internal target for service reliability or performance.I understand SLOs as measurable service goals teams aim to meet.
SLAA formal service commitment to customers.I distinguish customer-facing SLAs from internal SLO targets.
CapacityAvailable resources to handle demand.I treat capacity as a planning and reliability concern.
HeadroomRemaining capacity before risk increases.I use headroom to discuss risk before saturation happens.
ForecastAn estimate of future behavior based on trends.I use forecasts to support planning, not as perfect predictions.
Risk BandA simple low/medium/high framing of operational risk.I use risk bands to communicate priority clearly to leadership.
OpenTelemetryA standard way to collect and move telemetry such as metrics, logs, and traces.I understand OpenTelemetry as the modern vendor-neutral telemetry collection layer, and I am building practical experience with it.
CollectorA component that receives, processes, and exports telemetry.I understand the collector role as telemetry routing and transformation.
PrometheusA metrics and time-series monitoring system.I understand Prometheus as a common metrics platform and I am building hands-on depth.
GrafanaA platform for dashboards and observability visualization.I understand Grafana as a visualization layer and I am building practical usage.
AWS CloudWatchAWS service for metrics, logs, alarms, and dashboards.I understand CloudWatch as AWS-native monitoring and I am building applied experience.
DynatraceAn observability and APM platform.I can explain where Dynatrace fits and I am refreshing modern-platform knowledge.
SplunkA platform for log analytics and observability workflows.I can explain Splunk's role in logs and observability while building deeper practical skill.
DatadogA cloud observability platform across metrics, logs, and traces.I can explain Datadog's platform role and I am building hands-on capability progressively.
KubernetesA system for running and scaling containerized applications.I understand Kubernetes monitoring concepts and I am building practical operations depth.
PodThe smallest deployable compute unit in Kubernetes.I understand pod health as a core monitoring signal in Kubernetes.
NodeA worker machine that runs pods.I monitor node health to understand cluster capacity and stability risk.
ServiceA stable network endpoint for a set of pods.I treat service-level signals as key indicators of user-facing health.
NamespaceA logical scope boundary for Kubernetes resources.I use namespaces to separate monitoring views by team or workload scope.
