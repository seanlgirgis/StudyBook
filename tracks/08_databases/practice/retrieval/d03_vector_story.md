# Vector Retrieval — Story Map

## 1. Story (finding similar songs / movies / ideas by vibe)
You like a song with a chill vibe. Instead of matching exact words, you ask for songs that feel similar.

## 2. Core Concepts (street version)
- Words can be turned into vectors.
- Similar meaning = nearby vectors.
- Retrieval = find nearest neighbors.

## 3. What vector retrieval is
A search method that finds items by semantic similarity in vector space.

## 4. Why keyword search is not enough
Different words can mean the same thing, so exact matching can miss good results.

## 5. What embeddings are (simple intuition only)
Embeddings are numeric fingerprints of meaning.

## 6. Similarity / nearest neighbors
The closest vectors to a query are the best semantic matches.

## 7. What vector retrieval is great at
- Semantic matching
- Querying by intent
- Finding similar items

## 8. What vector retrieval is bad at
- Exact filters
- Precise factual lookups without structure

## 9. Final mental model
Meaning becomes a point in space. Nearest points are most similar.
