# Friction Scoring — JobSearch 2.0

Purpose:
Decide quickly whether to APPLY or SKIP a job based on likelihood of getting hired.

This is not about “can I do the job”.
This is about “will they say YES quickly”.

---

## Scoring Dimensions (0–2 each)

### 1. Hiring Speed
- 2 = Contract / urgent / “immediate hire”
- 1 = Normal full-time
- 0 = Slow enterprise / unclear timeline

---

### 2. Process Complexity
- 2 = Simple (1–2 interviews, no heavy coding)
- 1 = Moderate (3–4 steps, light coding)
- 0 = Heavy (proctored tests, long loops, FAANG-style)

---

### 3. Stack Match
- 2 = Strong match (AWS, Spark, Python, pipelines)
- 1 = Partial match
- 0 = Weak / unrelated stack

---

### 4. Overqualification Risk
- 2 = Matches your level (senior / contract / hands-on)
- 1 = Slight mismatch
- 0 = Likely seen as overqualified

---

### 5. Role Clarity
- 2 = Clear responsibilities and expectations
- 1 = Somewhat clear
- 0 = Vague / confusing / “do everything”

---

### 6. Compensation Fit
- 2 = Acceptable and realistic
- 1 = Slightly off but possible
- 0 = Clearly too low or unclear

---

## Total Score

Max score = 12

---

## Decision Rules

### APPLY (Fast Lane)
Score: 9–12

- Apply immediately
- Use full pipeline (tailored resume, cover)

---

### REVIEW (Manual Decision)
Score: 6–8

- Quick judgment:
  - “Do I realistically get an interview here?”
- If unsure → skip

---

### SKIP
Score: 0–5

- Do not apply
- Do not spend time

---

## Hard Skip Conditions (Override Score)

Skip immediately if:
- Proctored coding test required
- 5+ interview rounds
- FAANG-style hiring process
- Role is too junior with high experience mismatch

---

## Time Rule (Critical)

- Max 2–3 minutes per job (score + decision)
- If unclear after 3 minutes → SKIP
- Do not deep-read job descriptions before scoring

---

## Execution Rule

You must score every job before applying.

No exceptions.

---

## Weekly Calibration

At end of week:

- Which scored jobs led to responses?
- Which scores were misleading?

Adjust scoring based on real outcomes.

---

## Goal

Reduce:
- wasted applications
- time spent on low-probability roles

Increase:
- interview rate
- speed to offer
