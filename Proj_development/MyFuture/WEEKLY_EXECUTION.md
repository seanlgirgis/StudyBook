# Weekly Execution

## Weekly Review Structure
1. Outcomes
- What cash came in this week? (A/B/C)
- What shipped (applications, demos, shifts)?

2. Bottlenecks
- What slowed progress?
- Which tasks had the highest friction?

3. Decisions
- What to continue?
- What to pause or cut?
- What to test next week?

4. Next Week Priorities (Placeholder)
- Priority 1: TODO
- Priority 2: TODO
- Priority 3: TODO

5. Minimum Commitments
- Income Now: TODO shifts/tasks
- JobSearch 2.0: TODO applications/outreach
- AI Income: TODO demo/client actions

## Weekly Cadence
- Monday: set targets
- Midweek: adjust based on cash-flow reality
- Friday/Sunday: review, decide, and reset
