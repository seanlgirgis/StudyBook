# Weekly Execution

Purpose:
Turn strategy into daily action without overthinking.

---

## Daily Operating Rule

Every day must include:

- Track C (Income Now): earn or secure cash
- Track A (JobSearch): apply or outreach
- Track B (AI Income): build or sell

Even small actions count.

---

## Daily Structure (Default)

### Block 1 — Income First (Track C)
- Complete income task (Uber / delivery / shift)
- Stop when daily target is reached

---

### Block 2 — JobSearch 2.0 (Track A)
- Score jobs using friction scoring
- Apply only to high-score jobs (9–12)
- Target: 3–5 quality applications OR 2–3 outreach messages

---

### Block 3 — AI Income (Track B)
- Build demo OR
- Improve demo OR
- Do outreach

Target:
- Ship 1 small improvement OR
- Send 3–5 outreach messages

---

## Weekly Targets

### Track C — Income Now
- Hit weekly cash target ($$$)
- Do not miss

---

### Track A — JobSearch
- 15–25 high-quality applications
- 5 recruiter / direct contacts

---

### Track B — AI Income
- Week 1: working demo
- Week 2: outreach + conversations
- Week 3: first paid attempt

---

## Weekly Review (End of Week)

### 1. Outcomes
- Cash earned (Track C)
- Applications sent (Track A)
- Demo/outreach progress (Track B)

---

### 2. Bottlenecks
- What slowed progress?
- Where did time get wasted?

---

### 3. Decisions
- What to continue?
- What to stop?
- What to adjust?

---

### 4. Next Week Priorities
- Priority 1: (most important)
- Priority 2:
- Priority 3:

---

### 5. Minimum Commitments
- Income Now: ___ shifts/tasks
- JobSearch: ___ applications
- AI Income: ___ demo/outreach actions

---

## Adjustment Rules

- If cash target is missed → increase Track C time
- If no interviews after 2 weeks → adjust job targeting
- If first client is close → increase Track B time

---

## Anti-Rabbit Hole Rules

- Do not start new ideas
- Do not rebuild systems
- Do not chase perfection

Focus on:
- finishing
- shipping
- talking to real people

---

## Daily Close (2–3 minutes)

- What did I complete today?
- What is the first task tomorrow?

Write it down before stopping.
