# Strategy

## Objective
Stabilize income quickly while building a path to independent earning.

## Guiding Principles
- Cash flow first
- Control second
- Independence third
- Small wins over big plans
- Avoid overbuilding or overthinking

---

## Track Roles

### Track C — Income Now (Priority 1)
Purpose:
- Immediate cash flow to protect household

Definition of success:
- Weekly income target met

Weekly Target:
- $____ (DEFINE based on real needs)

Daily Minimum:
- $____ (stop when reached unless behind)

Rules:
- This track is non-negotiable
- Stop daily effort once target is reached
- Do not overwork beyond target

---

### Track A — JobSearch 2.0 (Priority 2)
Purpose:
- Secure stable income (contract or easier full-time)

Target roles:
- Contract roles
- Mid-size companies
- Low-friction hiring pipelines

Avoid:
- Heavy algorithm/proctored pipelines
- Overly competitive big tech roles

Definition of success:
- Interviews generated
- Offer pipeline started

---

### Track B — AI Income (Priority 3 → future Priority 1)
Purpose:
- Build independent income capability

Focus:
- ONE offer only (AI document/data assistant)

Definition of success:
- First paid client
- Repeatable workflow

Rules:
- No SaaS building
- No multiple ideas
- Demo → sell → refine

---

## Time Allocation Rules (Initial)

Default weekly allocation:
- Track C (Income Now): 40–60%
- Track A (JobSearch): 20–30%
- Track B (AI Income): 20–30%

Adjust based on cash pressure.

---

## Minimum Weekly Outputs

Track C:
- Meet income target ($$$)

Track A:
- 15–25 targeted applications
- 5 recruiter or direct contacts

Track B:
- Ship 1 demo improvement OR
- Have 3–5 outreach conversations

---

## Reassessment Triggers

Shift MORE time to Track C if:
- Weekly income target is missed

Shift MORE time to Track A if:
- No interviews after 2 weeks

Shift MORE time to Track B if:
- First client is acquired

Pause any track if:
- No measurable progress after 2 weeks

---

## First Milestone Targets

- Week 1–2:
  - Stable income routine (Track C)
  - Working AI demo (Track B)

- Week 2–4:
  - Interview pipeline started (Track A)
  - First client attempt (Track B)

---

## Execution Rule

Every day must include:
- Income action (C)
- Job search action (A)
- Build or outreach action (B)

Even small actions count.
Consistency beats intensity.
