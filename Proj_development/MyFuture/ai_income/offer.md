# Offer — AI Document & Data Assistant (V1)

## One-Line Offer
I build a simple AI system that lets your team search, ask questions, and get answers from your documents and data.

---

## Who This Is For (Initial Target)
- Small businesses with lots of documents (PDFs, reports, notes)
- Teams that waste time searching for information
- Non-technical users who want simple answers, not dashboards

Examples:
- Real estate teams
- Small consulting firms
- Operations teams
- Solo professionals

---

## Problem I Solve
- “We can’t find information quickly”
- “We spend too much time digging through files”
- “Knowledge is scattered across documents”

---

## What I Deliver (V1 Scope)

A working system that includes:

1. Document ingestion
- Upload PDFs, text, or basic files

2. AI Q&A
- Ask questions in plain English
- Get answers based on your documents

3. Basic setup
- Local or simple hosted setup
- Instructions for usage

4. Optional add-on (later)
- Daily/weekly summaries
- Email or report automation

---

## What I DO NOT Do (Important)
- No full SaaS platform
- No complex multi-agent systems
- No long custom builds

Keep it small, fast, and useful.

---

## Delivery Model

- Setup time: 2–5 days
- Simple demo first
- Then light customization for client data

---

## Pricing (Initial — to test)

Option A (fast start):
- $200 – $500 one-time setup

Option B (slightly higher value):
- $500 – $1000 setup depending on scope

Optional:
- Small monthly support ($50–$150)

NOTE:
Pricing is flexible until first 2–3 clients are closed.

---

## Acceptance Criteria (What “Done” Means)

- Client can upload documents
- Client can ask questions and get answers
- Answers are reasonably accurate
- Client understands how to use the system

---

## Demo Plan

Use one clear example:

Example demo:
- Upload sample documents (reports, PDFs)
- Ask:
  - “Summarize this”
  - “What are the key points?”
  - “Find X information”

Show:
- Speed
- Simplicity
- Usefulness
