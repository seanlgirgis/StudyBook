# Synthetic Demo Document

## Maintenance Plan Policy

## Plan Name
Comfort Care Plan (demo)

## Included Benefits
- Two HVAC tune-ups per year (spring and fall)
- Priority scheduling for HVAC calls
- Reduced diagnostic fee on covered equipment
- Filter and thermostat optimization checklist

## Enrollment Notes
- Membership starts after first paid visit.
- Plans are household-based and non-transferable.
- Cancel any time before next renewal cycle.

## Edge Cases
- Missed tune-up windows can be rescheduled within same season based on capacity.
- Plan discount does not combine with all coupons.
