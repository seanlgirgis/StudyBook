## Plumbing Services Policy

## Included Plumbing Work
- Drain clearing
- Leak checks and minor line repair
- Fixture replacement (faucets, toilets, disposals)
- Shutoff valve and pressure diagnostics

## Not Included
- Slab re-piping projects
- City-permit-only sewer reconstruction
- Specialty well-system service

## Scheduling Priority
- Active leak with property-damage risk: urgent queue.
- Non-urgent fixture issue: first available standard slot.

## Handoff Rule
If request is outside plumbing scope, fallback with out-of-scope message and offer referral list callback.
