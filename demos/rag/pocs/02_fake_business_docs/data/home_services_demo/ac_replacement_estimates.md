## A/C Replacement Estimate Policy

## When To Recommend Estimate Discussion
Recommend replacement estimate review when one or more are true:
- unit age is 15+ years
- major component failure with high repair cost
- repeated breakdowns in a single cooling season
- utility costs rising with weak cooling performance

## Repair vs Replace Guidance
- If estimated repair is below 30% of replacement and no repeat-history: repair-first recommendation.
- If estimated repair exceeds 40% of replacement on 15+ year unit: replacement-first recommendation.
- Between 30-40%: present both options with pros/cons and no pressure language.

## Estimate Process
- Provide on-site inspection window.
- Gather equipment tonnage, duct condition, and comfort goals.
- Present at least two equipment options.

## Customer Communication Rule
Do not promise exact pricing in chat.
Use estimate range language and schedule on-site confirmation.
