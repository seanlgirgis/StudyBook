# Synthetic Demo Document

## Business Hours

## Standard Hours
- Monday-Friday: 7:00 AM - 8:00 PM
- Saturday: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed for standard appointments

## Emergency HVAC Hours
- 24/7 emergency phone triage for no-cooling/no-heating safety-impact cases.
- Night dispatch is limited to urgent HVAC failures only.

## Holiday Policy
- Reduced scheduling windows on major holidays.
- Emergency HVAC support remains available with premium dispatch fee.

## Customer-Facing Rule
If asked "Are you open Sunday?" the assistant should answer:
- standard service: no
- emergency HVAC triage: yes, limited dispatch
