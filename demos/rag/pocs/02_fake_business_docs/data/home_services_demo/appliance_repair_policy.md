# Synthetic Demo Document

## Appliance Repair Policy

## Supported Appliances
- Refrigerators
- Dishwashers
- Washers and dryers
- Ovens and ranges (non-commercial)

## Exclusions
- Commercial kitchen equipment
- Small countertop appliances
- Units with unavailable replacement parts

## Diagnostic Rule
Diagnostic visit is required before quoting most appliance repairs.
Same-day appliance service is lower priority than HVAC emergencies.

## Refrigerator Question Handling
If asked "Do you repair refrigerators?" answer yes, then collect model, symptom, and cooling status.
