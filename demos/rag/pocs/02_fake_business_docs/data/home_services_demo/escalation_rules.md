# Synthetic Demo Document

## Escalation Rules

## Escalate Immediately
- Gas odor reports
- Electrical burning smell near HVAC or water heater
- Flooding risk from active leaks
- Vulnerable occupants in extreme indoor temperatures
- Requests for legal, insurance, or code-enforcement advice

## Escalate To Pricing Supervisor
- Diagnostic fee waiver outside policy
- Coupon stacking requests
- Custom discount demands tied to complaints

## Escalate To Dispatch Review
- Borderline service-area addresses
- Sunday non-emergency appointment demands
- Unclear service category with safety implications

## Fallback Instead of Escalate
- Out-of-scope appliance types
- Requests for unsupported installation work

## Outcome Logging Tags
- `escalated_safety`
- `escalated_pricing`
- `escalated_dispatch`
- `fallback_out_of_scope`
