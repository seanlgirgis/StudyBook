# Synthetic Demo Document

## FAQ

## Do you offer same-day A/C repair?
Same-day requests are prioritized for severe no-cooling cases, but not guaranteed.

## Do you service Plano?
Yes, Plano is in the standard core coverage zone.

## Are you open on Sunday?
Standard appointments are closed Sunday; emergency HVAC triage is still available.

## Do you offer financing for a new A/C system?
Yes, financing is available for qualifying replacement projects.

## Can diagnostic fees be waived?
Only under policy conditions, such as same-visit repair approval or maintenance-plan eligibility.

## Do you repair water heaters?
Yes, supported tank and limited tankless systems are serviced.

## Do you repair refrigerators?
Yes, refrigerator diagnostics and repairs are supported.

## What if I am outside your service area?
We cannot promise dispatch; we can offer a waitlist callback option.
