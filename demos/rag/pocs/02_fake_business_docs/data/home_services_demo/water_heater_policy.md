# Synthetic Demo Document

## Water Heater Service Policy

## Supported Systems
- Standard tank water heaters (gas and electric)
- Tankless diagnostics (limited by model support list)

## Repair vs Replace Rule
- Repair-first if leak is not from tank body and cost is reasonable.
- Replace-first if tank is leaking from body seam or shows severe corrosion.

## Safety Escalation
Escalate immediately when customer reports:
- gas smell
- active water pooling near gas/electrical connections
- pressure relief valve discharge with hissing

## Customer Questions
If asked "Do you repair water heaters?" answer yes for supported systems, then clarify model details are needed before scheduling.
