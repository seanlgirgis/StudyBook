# Synthetic Demo Document

## HVAC Repair Policy

## Repair Eligibility
HVAC repair is offered when:
- the system can be safely restored,
- parts are available within 1-3 business days, and
- estimated repair cost is below replacement-threshold guidance.

## Same-Day Service Target
- Same-day appointments are prioritized for no-cooling above 88F outdoor temperature.
- Same-day is not guaranteed; dispatch depends on slot availability and location.

## Not-Cooling Triage Steps
1. Confirm thermostat mode and setpoint.
2. Confirm power status and breaker state.
3. Check airflow at main vents.
4. Ask for unusual sounds, odors, or water around indoor unit.

## Urgency Detection Rules
Escalate to urgent scheduling when:
- elderly or medically sensitive occupants report extreme indoor heat/cold
- complete system outage in severe weather
- burning odor or electrical hazard indicators

## Repair Deferral Rule
If unsafe electrical, gas, or refrigerant concerns are suspected, skip DIY advice and escalate to dispatcher immediately.
